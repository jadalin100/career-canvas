A simple pilot partnership

Give students a real audience for their ideas.

Career Canvas students research your business, publish a business article, create an educational quiz, and design an original advertisement for you. You receive every finished piece.

What students create

Four connected deliverables

  1. A one-to-four-page article about your business and audience
  2. An educational quiz connected to your industry
  3. An original flyer, social post, or short video concept
  4. A portfolio story that credits your partnership

What we ask from you

A short introduction and clear brief

  1. Introduce the business by video clip or short paragraph
  2. Complete the student details form
  3. Share advertisement ideas you would genuinely use
  4. Optional: offer brief feedback on student work
