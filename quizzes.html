Who we are

We're Jada Lin and Olivia Zheng, two DECA students at Great Neck South. This site started as our DECA Career Development Project — most "career exploration" for business students is a 20-minute assembly or a worksheet nobody reads twice, so we built something people would actually use.

What we're trying to do

Give students a real way into business careers: what a marketing job actually looks like day to day, which digital branding work fits different creative strengths, and which DECA event matches how you think. Right now that's three quizzes — more tools are coming as we build them.
