Our mission

A win-win: students create real work they can show, while local businesses receive original ads and community impact.

The journey

Student program
pipeline.

Five connected stages turn a student’s strengths into published research, an interactive quiz, a real business ad, and a portfolio.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find your fit.

    Discover your DECA event fit, career interests, and digital branding strengths.

  2. 02

    Research

    Publish what you learn.

    Research one local business partner and publish a one-to-four-page business article to our site.

  3. 03

    Build

    Create a quiz app.

    Turn the business topic into an educational quiz that classmates can play.

  4. 04

    Create

    Design a real ad.

    Create an original business ad that the partner can actually use.

  5. 05

    Showcase

    Make your growth visible.

    Present your work and new experience in a personalized digital portfolio and resume.
