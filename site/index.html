The journey

Student program
pipeline

Five connected stages turn a student’s strengths into published research, a real business ad, an interactive quiz, and a portfolio.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find your fit

    Discover your DECA event fit, career interests, and digital marketing strengths.

  2. 02

    Research

    Publish what you learn

    Research one local business and publish a one-to-four-page business article to our site.

  3. 03

    Create

    Design a real ad

    Create an original ad for the business you researched.

  4. 04

    Build

    Create a quiz app

    Turn the business topic into an educational quiz that classmates can play.

  5. 05

    Showcase

    Make your growth visible

    Present your work and new experience in a personalized digital portfolio and resume.
