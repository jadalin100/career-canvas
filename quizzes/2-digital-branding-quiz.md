# Quiz — "Which Part of Digital Branding Fits You?"

*Career Canvas · a creative business quiz for middle school students*

**How it works:** Choose the task you would most want to own. Ten questions.
At the end, you will see the three digital-branding areas that best match how
you like to think and create.

---

## The Questions

**Q1. A new café asks your team for help. What would you do first?**
- A) Decide what the café should be known for. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) Sketch a logo and choose a color direction. `[VISUAL]`
- C) Write the story behind the café and its menu. `[CONTENT]`
- D) Plan how the café should talk with students online. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) Design a short quiz that recommends a drink. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q2. Which blank page feels most exciting?**
- A) A brand-positioning worksheet with one big question. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) An empty artboard waiting for a poster. `[VISUAL]`
- C) A document waiting for a headline and story. `[CONTENT]`
- D) A content calendar with space for daily ideas. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) A screen where someone will click, choose, and get a result. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q3. Your campaign is not connecting. What do you investigate?**
- A) Whether the idea solves the right audience problem. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) Whether the design is clear and recognizable. `[VISUAL]`
- C) Whether the message sounds specific and human. `[CONTENT]`
- D) Whether the brand is listening and responding to people. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) Whether the experience is easy and satisfying to use. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q4. Which compliment would mean the most to you?**
- A) "That idea fits the business perfectly." `[STRATEGY]`
- B) "I recognized the brand before I saw its name." `[VISUAL]`
- C) "That sentence made me want to keep reading." `[CONTENT]`
- D) "People actually joined the conversation." `[SOCIAL]`
- E) "I wanted to click through the whole thing." `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q5. Pick the tool you would most want to learn.**
- A) A research board for organizing audience insights. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) A design tool for layouts, colors, and typography. `[VISUAL]`
- C) A writing board for scripts, captions, and articles. `[CONTENT]`
- D) A social platform dashboard for planning and measuring posts. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) A no-code builder for websites, games, or quizzes. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q6. A local store is launching a new product. Which job do you claim?**
- A) Define the audience, promise, and reason to believe. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) Create the package and launch graphics. `[VISUAL]`
- C) Write the product name, description, and launch script. `[CONTENT]`
- D) Run the countdown, comments, and community responses. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) Build an online product finder that guides customers. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q7. Which detail do you notice first on a website?**
- A) Whether the whole experience has a clear purpose. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) The colors, type, images, and spacing. `[VISUAL]`
- C) The headline and the way the story unfolds. `[CONTENT]`
- D) The reviews, comments, and signs of a real community. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) The buttons, choices, motion, and response to clicks. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q8. Your team has one hour left. Where are you most useful?**
- A) Cutting ideas that do not support the main goal. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) Making every screen or page feel visually connected. `[VISUAL]`
- C) Tightening the words so the message is easy to remember. `[CONTENT]`
- D) Planning how to introduce the work and invite reactions. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) Testing every interaction and fixing confusing steps. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q9. Which experiment sounds most fun?**
- A) Interview five people and change the idea based on what you learn. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) Try three visual styles and see which one people remember. `[VISUAL]`
- C) Test three headlines and see which one makes people curious. `[CONTENT]`
- D) Try three post formats and compare the conversations they start. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) Build two versions of a quiz and watch where people get stuck. `[INTERACTIVE]`

**Q10. Which finished project would you be proudest to show?**
- A) A brand plan that gives a business a clear direction. `[STRATEGY]`
- B) A visual identity that looks distinctive everywhere it appears. `[VISUAL]`
- C) A campaign story that people quote and share. `[CONTENT]`
- D) A community campaign that gets people involved. `[SOCIAL]`
- E) A polished website or quiz people enjoy using. `[INTERACTIVE]`

---

## Your Results

- **Brand Strategy** `[STRATEGY]` — You like finding the clearest direction before anyone starts making things. You would enjoy audience research, positioning, campaign planning, and deciding what a brand should stand for.
- **Visual Identity and Design** `[VISUAL]` — You think through color, type, images, and layout. You would enjoy logos, campaign graphics, art direction, packaging, and building a recognizable visual system.
- **Content and Storytelling** `[CONTENT]` — You care about the words and ideas that make people pay attention. You would enjoy headlines, scripts, articles, captions, interviews, and shaping a brand voice.
- **Social Media and Community** `[SOCIAL]` — You like helping a brand participate in real conversations. You would enjoy content planning, community engagement, creator partnerships, and learning what audiences respond to.
- **Interactive Experience** `[INTERACTIVE]` — You want people to do something, not only look. You would enjoy websites, quiz apps, no-code tools, user experience, prototypes, and testing how an experience works.

---

**Scoring:** each answer adds **+1** to the area in brackets. Show the top
three areas. Ties keep the result order above.
