# Quiz 1 — "What Business Major Fits You?"

*Career & College Compass · a Futurepreneurs quiz for middle schoolers*

**How it works:** Pick the answer that sounds *most* like you — go with your gut,
there are no wrong answers. 30 questions, about 6–7 minutes. At the end you'll get
**your top 3 matches**.

> **Builder's note:** with 14 possible majors, this quiz needs all 30 questions to
> separate them. Each major appears in **8 or 9 answer options**, and options are
> **shuffled** so no major is always in the same position. If you edit questions,
> keep both properties — otherwise one major quietly eats all the results.
> Codes in `[brackets]` are for scoring only; students never see them.

---

## Your Results (14 possible majors)

- **Marketing** `[MKT]` — You sell the *why-you'll-love-it*. Ads, branding, social, and getting people to care. → *Try DECA's marketing role-plays.*
- **Finance** `[FIN]` — You make money grow and spot smart risks. Investing, markets, planning. → *Business Finance; Stock Market Game.*
- **Accounting** `[ACC]` — The truth-teller of business. Precise, trusted, detail-loving. → *Accounting Applications.*
- **Management / Business Administration** `[MGT]` — The one who runs the whole thing and keeps a team moving. → *Principles of Business Management.*
- **Entrepreneurship & Innovation** `[ENT]` — You build things that didn't exist yet and bet on your ideas. → *Entrepreneurship events.*
- **Hospitality & Tourism Management** `[HOS]` — You create experiences people remember — hotels, events, travel. → *Hospitality role-plays.*
- **Economics** `[ECON]` — You see the big invisible forces behind prices, choices, and markets. → *Great base for finance + policy.*
- **Business Analytics / Data** `[ANLY]` — You find the story hidden in the numbers and predict what's next. → *The future of every business.*
- **Human Resources Management** `[HR]` — You build great teams and keep workplaces fair and happy. → *Human Resources Management event.*
- **International Business** `[INTL]` — You do business across borders, languages, and cultures. → *Perfect for global brands.*
- **Supply Chain & Operations Management** `[SCM]` — You make the whole machine run — right thing, right place, fast and cheap. → *The behind-the-scenes hero.*
- **Management Information Systems (MIS)** `[MIS]` — You're the bridge between tech and business, solving problems with systems. → *Business + coding.*
- **Real Estate** `[RE]` — You see value in property, location, and the deal. → *Buying, selling, and building.*
- **Sports & Entertainment Management** `[SEM]` — You love the game *and* the business behind it — teams, athletes, shows. → *Sports & Entertainment Marketing.*
