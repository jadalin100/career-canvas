# Career Development Project (DECA)

**Partner:** Olivia · **School:** Great Neck South · **Event:** DECA Career
Development Project (project-management written entry + 15-minute presentation).

## The idea (one line)
A student-run career-development initiative that connects Great Neck / NYC
students to real local businesses — through a cold-email interview pipeline, mock
interviews, resume workshops, and a career fair — packaged as a DECA Career
Development Project.

## Goal & scope (DECA "initiating")
- **Purpose:** give students real exposure to careers in marketing, finance,
  hospitality, management, and entrepreneurship.
- **Deliverables:** a cold-email interview series, a mock-interview + resume
  workshop, a career fair, and (optional) a Green Thumbs Club + volunteer-finder
  tool.
- **Expected benefit:** students gain contacts, interview practice, and career
  clarity; local businesses get visibility with young talent.
